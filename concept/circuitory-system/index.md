# circuitory-system
> the flow structure that keeps a sprite coherent

the CIRCUITORY SYSTEM is the looping flow of thaum inside a sprite.
it looks like a mock of biology because it borrows patterns from its source.
its function is not breathing or pumping. its function is to keep thaum in a steady loop.

---
## WHAT WE HAVE SEEN
- a closed circuit allows organell to fire at any moment
- ruptured circuits lead to sluggish or partial behavior

## WHAT TO WATCH FOR
- familiar shapes can mislead. the same form does not mean the same purpose
