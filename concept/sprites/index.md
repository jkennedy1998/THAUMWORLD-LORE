# sprites
> thaumic life that forms from saturated biological sources

SPRITES are thaumic anomalies that form when there is so much magic from biological sources that it imprints on the world around it.
the imprint gathers living matter into a pseudo body. the body reflects the local ecology, not a single original creature.

SPRITES are new minds. they are not old minds in new shells.

---
## WHAT WE HAVE SEEN
- sprites appear after mass death, plague, or long decay where thaum saturates the soil and air
- bodies are stitched by environment: bark, bone, moss, rot, river silt, stone dust
- many sprites imitate the shapes of local predators or pack creatures

## WHAT WE THINK CAUSES IT
- dense biological thaum floods an area and binds to life-patterns in nearby matter
- the environment supplies the material while the thaum supplies the pattern

## WHAT TO WATCH FOR
- behaviors are new, not inherited, even when the body looks familiar
- anatomy is often elegant at a distance and nonsensical up close
