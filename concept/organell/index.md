# organell
> functional nodes within a sprite

ORGANELL are functional nodes in a sprite’s circuitory system.
they are not organs. they are ignition points and gates for thaum flow.

---
## WHAT WE HAVE SEEN
- organell are inherited from many sources: dragons, foliage, even structures
- mixed sources often produce organs that lead nowhere
- dead-end organell contribute to individual quirks and behavior
