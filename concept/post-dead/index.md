# post-dead
> an old mind in a new body

POST DEAD are preserved minds that rebuild or claim new bodies.
they keep identity continuity. they are not newly formed consciousness.

---
## HOW THEY ARE CONFUSED
- sprite bodies can resemble the dead or agglomerates of the dead
- witnesses often assume any reanimated shape is a post dead

## COMMON DISTINCTIONS
- a post dead speaks and remembers with continuity
- a sprite may mimic but does not recall a life it never had
