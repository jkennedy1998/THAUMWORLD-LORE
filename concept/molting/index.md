# molting
> a living mind shifting from biology to thaumic motion

MOLTING is the name for a living being who keeps going by magical or mechanical means as the body fails.
it is a brief and unstable way to live. without stabilization, the state collapses into death or a different form.

---
## WHAT WE HAVE SEEN
- elderly mages sustaining motion with thaum while their organs fail
- wounded casters binding nerve pathways with resonance to delay death

## WHAT TO WATCH FOR
- molting is not a new mind and not a new body
- prolonged molting erodes identity or causes uncontrolled metamorphosis
