THAUMWORLD — LORE MEMORY (CANONICAL)

WORLD NAMING

- THAUMWORLD is the current, primary name of the setting.
- Galadria is the old name for THAUMWORLD.
- In-universe usage:
  - “Galadria” appears in historical texts, myths, early scholarship, and older cultures.
  - “THAUMWORLD” is the modern scholarly/global term.
- Both names refer to the same world and cosmology.

STATUS: Canonical


CORE COSMOLOGY

THAUM
- Thaum is an additional fundamental particle saturating most of the universe.
- Thaum flows through all living things.
- Thaum “keeps its shape” while flowing, preserving structure rather than diffusing randomly.
- Thaum can bind to concepts (physical, abstract, informational).
- Thaum is the substrate of magic, life, and consciousness.

CONCEPT CAPTURE
- Thaum naturally binds to concepts when repetition, coherence, or concentration is sufficient.
- Example concepts:
  - Fire / heat (stars)
  - Thoughts
  - Genetic code
  - Sound
  - Electrical charge
  - A single consciousness

THAUM STORAGE
- Thaum can be stored in crystalline structures.
- Stored thaum is inert but intact.
- Destabilizing the crystal releases thaum.
- Magic is defined by controlling thaum input (storage) and output (release).

CONSCIOUSNESS
- Consciousness is a thaumic phenomenon.
- It is a self-reinforcing conceptual structure of thaum.
- Regions poor in thaum have diminished or absent consciousness.

DEAD ZONES
- Areas with extremely low thaum saturation.
- Life is difficult or impossible.
- Earth exists near the lower bound of thaum saturation compared to THAUMWORLD.

STATUS: Canonical


DEITIES & CONCEPTUAL ASCENSION

DEITY FORMATION
- When a concept becomes sufficiently concentrated and self-reinforcing, a deity may emerge.
- Deities are usually not intentionally created.
- They emerge from resonance, repetition, and accumulation.

CRAFT DEITIES
- A master craftsman who becomes the pinnacle of a craft may ascend into a local deity.
- Resonates with practitioners of that craft.
- Knowledge transfer occurs via thaumic resonance.
- Similar concepts resonate like tuned bells.
- Distant or past deities may weakly resonate across space or time.

ELEMENTAL DEITIES
- A mage storing massive quantities of a concept (e.g., fire) may ascend.
- Appears to summon the element but actually releases stored concept-thaum.
- Other gods of the same concept can sense the new resonance.

TIERS OF GODS
- Minor gods: local phenomena
- Major gods: continental or planetary
- Cosmic gods: stellar or galactic scale

RUNES
- One rune exists for every consciousness.
- Every god has at least one rune.
- Some gods have multiple runes representing partitioned awareness.

GODLY IDENTITY
- Usually one primary consciousness controls a god.
- Sub-minds or aspects may exist.
- Control is not always unified.

STATUS: Canonical


THAUM–CRYSTAL INTERACTION & CASTING

RESONANT RELEASE
- Thaum is released from crystals via sound-based resonance.
- Pitch, amplitude, and harmonic structure matter.
- Poor resonance causes inefficiency, instability, or damage.

LIMITATION
- Magic is about controlling resonance, not creating power.
- Over-resonance causes uncontrolled discharge.
- Under-resonance fails to release thaum.

POWER SOURCES
- Living beings store thaum internally.
- Internal casting is limited and risky at high output.
- External crystalline batteries allow safer, higher, repeatable output.
- Low-tech casters may rely on voice, percussion, or environment.

THAUM EQUILIBRIUM
- Thaum diffuses toward equilibrium when materials touch.
- Enables passive charging and environmental bleed.
- Every atom carries thaum particles.

STATUS: Canonical


RESONANCE PRECISION & ORGANIC EXCEPTIONS

PRECISION REQUIREMENTS
- Effective resonance requires extreme precision.
- Minor deviations cause failure, bleed, chaos, or crystal damage.

WHY CRYSTALS DOMINATE
- Crystals support stable, repeatable resonance modes.
- Narrow frequency bands and predictable harmonics.

ORGANIC LIMITATION
- Organic matter almost never achieves required precision.
- Biological systems are noisy and damped.

EXCEPTION EVENTS
- Rare organic resonance events may occur:
  - Impossible vocal harmonics
  - Bone cavity resonance
  - Neural or muscular feedback loops
- These events are dangerous and extraordinary.

CULTURAL INTERPRETATION
- Considered miracles, divine signs, or proto-ascension events.

STATUS: Canonical


SPRITES, MOLTING, & PROTO-THAUMIC LIFE

SPRITES
- Emergent thaumic lifeforms.
- Form from extreme concentrations of biologically sourced thaum.
- Not undead, constructs, or elementals.
- Bodies are pseudo-biological assemblages shaped by environment.

FORMATION
- Biological thaum imprints life-patterns onto matter.
- Environment assembles a body reflecting its ecology.

MOLTING
- Transition from biological to thaumic existence.
- Inherently unstable and temporary.
- Without stabilization, entities collapse, disperse, or transform.

CIRCUITORY SYSTEM
- Sprites possess a thaumic circuitory system.
- Maintains continuous thaum flow.
- Organell act as functional nodes, not organs.

INDIVIDUALITY
- Sprite anatomy reflects environmental history.
- Dead circuits and malformed pathways create unpredictability.

SPRITES VS POST DEAD
- Post Dead preserve identity continuity.
- Sprites are new consciousnesses.
- Visual similarity often causes misclassification.

STATUS: Canonical


KINDS & ENTITY CLASSIFICATION

BIOLOGICAL KINDS
- Naked Apes: adaptable, culturally dominant generalists
- Maned Apes: heavier-built, expressive, territorial
- Wooly Apes: cold-adapted, resilient
- Snow Snoutlings: cold-burrowing rodent folk
- Hornlings: beetle kinds with defensive adaptations

NOTE
- These are biological beings.
- Thaumic traits are secondary, not origin-defining.

POST-BIOLOGICAL ENTITIES
- Golems: artificial thaumic constructs
- Post Dead: preserved consciousness with identity continuity
- Sprites: emergent thaumic life

REGISTRATION
- Cities maintain separate registries for:
  - Biological citizens
  - Constructed entities
  - Emergent thaumic life

STATUS: Canonical


GOLEM CITIES & HOSTILE ECOLOGIES

GOLEM CITIES
- Arise naturally in hostile environments.
- Favor non-biological persistence.
- Are uncommon but not anomalous.

WHY THEY FORM
- Golems tolerate toxins, radiation, extreme cold, and dead zones.
- Hostile environments filter out biological sapients.

SNOW–FOG GOLEM CITY
- Exists in extreme cold, fog, chemical hostility, and monster presence.
- Thrives because others cannot survive there.

INTERSTELLAR PATTERN
- Golem cities are convergent solutions.
- Appear near dead zones, ruins, wastelands, or catastrophe sites.

CULTURAL PERCEPTION
- Biological civilizations find them ominous or troubling.
- Golem cities find this irrelevant.

STATUS: Canonical
