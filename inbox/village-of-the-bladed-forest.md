## RAW NOTES
- the bladed forest is home to very tall bushes with big straight, flat leaves that look like blades
- the plant is carbon-based and unique to the land
- like agave, but with thinner leaves and taller/longer spanning
- the spiky, flimsy aloe/agave-like plants are harvested and woven into materials for clothes, insulated homes, and decorative cloth
