## nutshell
land of snow and fog is a major continental map of gorath.
it currently contains the written regions bladed forest, dry plains, and sea of clouds.

---

## regions

### [bladed forest](./bladed%20forest/)
a cold, wet forest known for its unrelenting and dangerous winters.

### [dry plains](./dry%20plains/)
broad plains of grasses and low vegetation, known for grazing herds and open country.

### [sea of clouds](./sea%20of%20clouds/)
a frigid mountainous region within the south east of the land of fog and snow.
