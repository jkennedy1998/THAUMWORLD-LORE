## green mage
the green mage is a notable local mage associated with the greenhat.

the green mage is a post dead who considers themself a sprite.
they remain capable, but live under the fear of mental degeneration and forgotten memory.

their work includes attempts to stabilize long-term degeneration and preserve consciousness across bodies.
