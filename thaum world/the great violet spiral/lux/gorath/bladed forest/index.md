# bladed forest

## nutshell
the bladed forest is known for its large spiked foliage and dangerous winters.
during the cold months, the forest hardens into a hazardous landscape of frozen blades, making travel difficult and often deadly.

those who live here survive by cutting and maintaining paths through the growth.
the village of the bladed forest welcomes travelers willing to help keep those routes open.
