# settlement checklist

Lean settlement prep checklist routed to the repo facets.
Use what matters. Do not force every field.

## index
- original purpose
- current purpose
- population
- general class
- name origin

## geography
- water
- land utilization
- town sections

## culture
- economy
- family
- travelers
- religion
- language
- death ritual
- social ritual
- social taboo
- legend
- views on government
- drug views

## life
- race
- sickness

## technology
- spring food
- summer food
- fall food
- winter food
- clothes
- exports
- imports
- defenses
- infrastructure
- construction
- pseudomagics

## thaumatry
- magic
- religion when local practice is the focus

## characters
- notable individuals if needed

## places
- named districts
- major buildings
- sub-locations

## science
- use only when the settlement needs discovered-system explanation

## note
Some fields can live in more than one facet depending on focus.
Example:
- religion as social practice -> `culture`
- religion as local magical practice -> `thaumatry`
- food as trade/social pattern -> `culture`
- food as preservation/infrastructure/material survival -> `technology`
