# dry plains notes

This file holds dry plains material that is not yet ready for canon pages.
Grounded basics have been moved into `thaum world/the great violet spiral/lux/gorath/dry plains/index.md`.

## current canonized points
- dry plains is a `map`
- it lies south of the bladed forest and north of the frozen desert
- it is larger than the bladed forest
- it is cold, dry, and non-uniform
- plainsnest is the largest written settlement on the map
- plainsnest is a black coastling city about the size of cloud breach castle
- black coastlings and naked apes are important written populations here
- the east is wetter from mountain runoff
- trade, working travel, hired armies, and monster hunters use the map routes
- wildfire is a major regional hazard
- wood and thatch are more common here than in cloud breach castle
- fire magic has meaningful associations with the region

## unfinished notes to sort later

### scale and travel feel
- about twice as wide as the bladed forest
- about as tall on the map as the bladed forest
- about 1 to 1.5 days to cross vertically
- about 3 to 5 days to cross horizontally
- broad travel area rather than a single social cluster

### borders and neighboring maps
- mixed transition zone with the bladed forest
- visible shift where sharp foliage becomes more common toward the forest
- `coastal forests` lies to the northeast
- `city of the eastward sea` is on the coastal forests map
- coastal forests borders both the dry plains and the bladed forest
- `high marshlands` lies on the opposite side
- `town of the high marsh` is there
- a mining village in the frozen desert lies relatively near to the south

### ecology
- more forager / grazing based than the bladed forest
- many sedges
- low foliage
- taller sparse foliage in the central and eastern areas
- ecological blur between dry plains grasses and bladed forest sharp foliage
- the dry plains may be part of the grazing pressure that drove the bladed forest's rigid leaf adaptations
- burrowing or boring creatures may survive wildfire by going underground

### settlement notes
- there are other smaller settlements besides plainsnest
- plainsnest has sister settlements and smaller local population clusters around it
- plainsnest is not the capital for the wider black coastling population
- `city of the eastward sea` is a capital population center for both black coastlings and naked apes

### economy and daily life
- herding and cattle farming are important
- herds are dangerous and not fully docile
- some farming exists in wetter regions
- food is more plentiful and less strained than in cloud breach castle
- lots of trade likely moves through the city of the eastward sea

### fire and thaumatry
- wildfires often run in stripes from west to east
- plainsnest likely uses architecture shaped around wildfire survival
- possible strong fire-mage presence
- possible fire sprites and other life adapted to heat plus arctic cold
- possible wild god tied to the fire rune
- the fire rune may stay the same while its holder changes over time

### artifact thread
- the party found another party group that used fire magic from this region
- this is the main current link from the abandoned snow hall haven thread to the dry plains
- this does not imply direct snow hall population history with the dry plains
