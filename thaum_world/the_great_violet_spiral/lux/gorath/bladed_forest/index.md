## nutshell
the bladed forest is known for its distinctive large spikey foliage.
during the winter months, the sharp frozen foliage becomes a hazard.
travelers often avoid these areas in winter months,
though the village of the bladed forests carves paths through the foliage,
and has always welcomed those who put the work to maintain these paths.
