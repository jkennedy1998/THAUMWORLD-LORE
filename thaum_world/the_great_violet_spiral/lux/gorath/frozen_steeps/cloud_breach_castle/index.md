# cloud_breach_castle
> a medium sized settlement built into the side of a mountain, overlooking the frozen steeps to the west, and the snow plains of the east.

> > scale : region

> > ages : proto_settlement, homestead, age_of_honing, age_of_expansion, age_of_class

> > facets : none

---

## nutshell 

cloudbreach castle is a medium sized settlement with neighborhoods, surrounding farmlands, layered wall defences, 
and a large expansive castle that towers over the surrounding area.
cloud breach castle is a secondary city, and is not the largest city by far.
it is a major hub for trade, is a military power, and is the self proclaimed capitol (and the largest settlement) of the frozen steeps.


## government

the castle has a feudal government, with a lord at the top, 
and various vassals and knights below him. 
the lord is responsible for the defense of the castle, and the surrounding lands, 
as well as the administration of justice and the collection of taxes. 

military is provided for the frozen steeps by the castle,
though often armies are occupied with beasts of all types in the frozen steeps.

## tiered_city

the city is tiered, with the castles at the top, 
the noble and wealthy neighborhoods below that, 
the middle class, trademen, and markets below that,
and the poor and working class at the bottom, near the farmlands and the walls.
