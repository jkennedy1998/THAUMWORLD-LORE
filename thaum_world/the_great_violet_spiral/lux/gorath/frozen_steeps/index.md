## nutshell
the frozen steeps are a humid, fridged and fertil soiled land ripe with varieties of plants, insect and mamal life. 
the notable settlements within the frozen steeps are thunderspire, cloudbreach castle, cloud gate outpost,and mason's rest at the foot of the mountains.
there is an abandoned snow hall havel that a snow bore beetle group used to occupy, but it has since been overrun by ice drakes, and wolf ants.
