## nutshell 

the thunderspire outpost started as a small religious community in the frozen steeps,
dedicated to the worship of a local diety of lightning and thunder.
over time, the community grew and evolved, becoming a hub for curious mages looking to study the unique properties of elecricity.

## environmental_management

the village is largely intelectual and spiritual workers, 
rellying on inports of food and supplies from the cloudbreach castle to the south, and plentiful trade routs of the north.
thunderspire's main export is the knowledge and research produced by the mages, which is funded in resources by the church, local government, and a few helpful patrons / sister settlements.
