# chlorine-life
> chlorine based organisms of the land of fog and snow

these organisms populate the LAND OF FOG AND SNOW.
they are often described as plant-bug-mushroom things because their forms refuse easy classification.
the name chlorine life is common in field notes.

---
## WHAT WE HAVE SEEN
- long rods, chitinous armor, exoskeletons, geometric growths
- malleable chitin and balloon-like bodies
- hides are cold, tough, and uncomfortable to the touch

## THAUM CONDUCTIVITY
- most are quite unconductive to thaum
- corpses and harvested materials resist magic naturally

## MATERIALS
- bodies are chlorine based
- their materials resemble plastics
- remains are often light and waterproof

## ORIGIN NOTES
- thought to be a byproduct of abundant sodium and chlorine left by space age ocean desalination
