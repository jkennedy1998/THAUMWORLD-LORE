## TEXT
WEIRD PLANT-BUG-MUSHROOM THINGS POPULATE THE LAND OF FOG AND SNOW

THE SHAPES THAT THESE KINDS TAKES IS LONG RODS, CHITTANOUS ARMOR, EXOSKELETONS, GEOMETRIC GROWTHS, AND MALUBLE LIKE CHITTIN, AND EVEN BALLOON LIKE CREATURES

THESE CREATURES ARE USUALLY QUITE UNCONDUCTIVE TO THAUM

THE HIDES OF THE CREATURES OF THIS TYPE ARE NOT COMFORTABLE A LOT OF THE TIME AND ARE OFTEN COLD AND TOUGH TO THE TOUCH.

THE CORPSES AND MATERIALS FROM THESE CREATURES RESIST MAGIC NATURALLY AND ARE OFTEN VERY LIGHT AND WATERPROOF.

THESE ORGANISMS ARE CHLORINE BASED AND THEIR MATERIALS ARE PLASTICS OFTEN. THE MAGICAL DAMPENING IMPLICATIONS OF THIS ARE COOL WITH HUMANS / NAKED APES.

COMMON THEORY SAYS THEY ARE A BYPRODUCT OF ABUNDANT SODIUM AND CHLORINE LEFT BY SPACE AGE OCEAN DESALINATION.
