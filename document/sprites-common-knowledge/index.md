## TEXT
SPRITES AND GOLEMS, FACTOIDS FROM COMMON KNOWLEDGE TO DEVELOPING SCIENCES.

SPRITES ARE THESE MAGIC ANOMALIES THAT FORM WHEN THERE SO MUCH MAGIC FROM BIOLOGICAL SOURCES
(IE: DEAD PEOPLE / ANIMALS)

THAT IT IMPRINTS ONTO THE WORLD AROUND IT, FORMING A PSEUDO BODY OUT OF LIVING MATTER.
(IE: SICK DEMON FOREST WOLVES AWOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOoooooooooo )

if you are living mostly through biological means and transfering to magic this is referred to as “molting”, but it is an ephemeral state of life.
(IE: a mage who is dying of old age but moves his body through mechanical or magical means)

SPRITES HAVE A “CIRCUITORY SYSTEM”, A SEEMINGLY MOCK OF BIOLOGICAL SYSTEMS WHERE IT SOURCED ITS MAGIC. THE SYSTEM FEATURES LOOK LIKE US, BUT THEIR PURPOSES ARE OFTEN QUITE DIFFERENT.

THE MAIN FUNCTION OF THE CIRCUITORY SYSTEM IS TO CREATE A LOOP OF MAGICAL ENERGY IN A FLOW STATE THAT GIVES ALL ORGANELL THE ABILITY TO FIRE AT ANY GIVEN TIME.

SPRITE ORGANELL CAN SOMETIMES BE INHERITED FROM ALL KINDS OF PLACES. DRAGONS, FOLIAGE, EVEN STRUCTURES!

SOMETIMES SPRITES FROM MIXED BIOLOGICAL SOURCES HAVE MIXED ORGANS THAT SEEM TO LEAD NOWHERE. THIS IS A CONTRIBUTOR TOWARDS INDIVIDUALITY IN SPRITE FORMS.

POST DEAD ARE OFTEN CONFUSED WITH SPRITES, BUT A POST DEAD IS AN OLD (ISH) BRAIN THAT HAS GIVEN ITSELF A NEW BODY,
AND A SPRITE IS A NEW BRAIN, WHOS BODY HAS THE SHAPE OF THE DEAD (OFTEN AN AGGLOMERATE OF THEM)
